## Ingredients

### Dough

- 1 cup (2 sticks) unsalted butter
- 1 cup packed brown sugar
- ½ cup granulated sugar
- 2 large eggs
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 2 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp kosher salt

### Mix-ins

- 1 1/2 cups chocolate chips
- Flaky salt, for topping

## Steps

1. Brown the butter in a light-colored pan over medium heat until it smells nutty and the solids turn golden, 5 to 7 minutes. Pour into a large bowl and cool 10 minutes.
2. Whisk in both sugars, then the eggs and vanilla.
3. Stir in the flour, baking soda, and salt until just combined. Fold in the chocolate chips.
4. Chill the dough for at least 30 minutes.
5. Heat the oven to 350°F. Scoop 2-tablespoon balls onto lined sheets, 2 inches apart.
6. Bake 10 to 12 minutes, until the edges are golden. Sprinkle with flaky salt while warm.

## Notes

Chilling overnight gives a deeper toffee flavor.
