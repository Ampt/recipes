## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 2 lb ground turkey
- 1 large onion, diced
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 1/2 tbsp chili powder
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 (28 oz) can crushed tomatoes
- 2 (15 oz) cans kidney beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 1/2 cups chicken broth
- 1-2 tsp salt, to taste

## Steps

1. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium-high heat. Brown the turkey, breaking it up, about 8 minutes.
2. Add the onion and pepper and cook until soft, 5 minutes. Stir in the garlic and spices for 1 minute.
3. Add the tomatoes, beans, and broth. Bring to a boil, then simmer uncovered for 30 minutes, stirring now and then.
4. Taste and add salt. Serve with cheddar, sour cream, and green onions.

## Notes

Better the next day. Freezes well for up to 3 months.
